Hi, I'm BAKKIS.

My music folders were huge and messy, and sorting them meant endless clicking and dragging. So I built the app I wanted: listen, swipe, next. No mouse needed.

It moves your tracks into tidy folders and removes the empty ones left behind. Every step can be undone.

Press H any time for the shortcuts.

Free and open source.
